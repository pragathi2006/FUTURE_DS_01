# FUTURE_DS_01
  
 ## 📊 Task 1 - Business Sales Performance Analytics

### 🎯 Objective
Analyzed business sales data to identify revenue trends, 
top-selling products, high-value categories, and regional 
performance using Power BI.

### 📁 Dataset
- Source: Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle)
- Records: 9,994 transactions
- Fields: Order Date, Region, Category, Product, Sales, Profit, Quantity

### 🔧 Tool Used
- Power BI Desktop

### 📈 Key Insights
- 💰 Total Revenue: 2.30M
- 📊 Total Profit: 286.40K
- 📦 Total Quantity Sold: 38K
- 🏆 Top Region: West has the highest sales
- 🥇 Top Category: Technology leads in revenue
- 🛒 Top Product: Canon imageCLASS is the best selling product

### 📊 Dashboard Contains
- KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Quantity)
- Sales by Region (Bar Chart)
- Sales by Category (Pie Chart)
- Sales Trend over Time (Line Chart)
- Top Products by Sales (Bar Chart)

### ✅ Deliverable
A client-ready Power BI dashboard with clear insights 
and actionable recommendations for business decisions.
